## How a label is bought

1. **Create** a shipment from one angler's ship boxes (one parcel per box), or a walk-in with its
   own measured parcels. It starts `draft` (or `held` when the ship date is past today + window)
   with the `address_unverified` blocker.
2. **Verify address** (`/api/shipping/verify`) → Shippo validate → `shipping_record_address`
   stores the carrier-normalised address or the `bad_address` blocker. A result for an address
   that changed meanwhile is ignored.
3. **Shop rates** (`/api/shipping/rates`): parcels are box weight + the size's packaging + dry ice
   (dry ice also declared to the carrier). Carriers outside `shipping.carrier_allowlist` are
   dropped (`*` = all). When the carrier set comes back **thin** — an active, allowed carrier
   neither quoted nor answered with an error; an empty answer counts — the shipment is re-quoted
   (up to 3 more times with back-off) and the fullest answer kept; silent carriers are shown.
4. **Choose** a rate (only one from the shipment's own stored shop — a Rate Check quote cannot be
   bought).
5. **Buy** (`/api/shipping/buy`): `begin_label_buy` evaluates the **I13 / I13b / I14** gate under
   the row lock (WP-15's `app.shipment_gate`). Held → reported as information *with* every real
   blocker, nothing bought. Manager **Buy Now** (PIN → elevation) lifts the date hold only;
   blockers, a missing ship date included, survive it. Otherwise a single-use **purchase claim**
   (id, 5-minute life, Buy Now flag) is issued; a second concurrent buy, a rate change, an edit
   or a cancel is refused while it lives. The claim is **not** permission to pay: a replayed
   `clientActionId` gets its stored claim back marked `replayed`.
   Before calling Shippo the route must **start the purchase** (`shipping_start_purchase`,
   service role): under the row lock it consumes the claim exactly once and re-checks the
   shipment as it is now (same claim, unexpired, still draft / held, rate chosen, gate still
   buys). So two requests with the same action id, or a replay after a failure / cancel, cannot
   start a second purchase.
   Shippo is then asked for existing transactions for the rate (adopted if any), else one
   purchase is made; **every** parcel's transaction is polled for and each ZPL label
   downloaded. `shipping_record_labels` accepts the labels only against the claim that started
   the purchase and refuses fewer labels than parcels. A failure reported by that handler
   releases the claim and keeps the reason; if the purchase had reached Shippo that is noted
   ("press Buy again to fetch the labels, or refund them in Shippo") and the next Buy adopts
   them. A stale handler's failure report is ignored.
   **The paid rate is pinned.** Starting a purchase records its rate as outstanding until the
   labels are recorded. Through failures and claim expiry nothing can change the rate: choosing
   a rate, a rate shop or address check answering mid-purchase, and editing the shipment are all
   refused. The next Buy therefore adopts the paid transaction instead of buying another rate.
   On failure the route asks Shippo whether anything exists for the rate; only "nothing" unpins
   it. Cancelling over an outstanding purchase needs a manager and notes a refund check.
6. **Print** (`print_shipment_labels`): one `shipping_label` ZPL job per parcel to the site's
   shipping-label printer. **Reprint is always allowed** once a label is bought — even for a
   future-dated or shipped shipment (P24).
7. **Shipped**: by hand (`mark_shipment_shipped`, refuses boxes not yet released at boxing) or by
   the tracking webhook when Shippo reports `TRANSIT`/`DELIVERED`; packed boxes → `shipped`;
   a `shipment_shipped` domain event is written for the email (WP-20). `RETURNED`/`FAILURE`
   are appended to the shipment's notes, never auto-resolved.

## Fixtures

The fixtures are Shippo responses in the API's documented shapes with test-mode ids and test
tracking numbers. They were **written to that shape, not captured from a live account** — no
Shippo key was available to this work package and none may be committed. Re-record them from a
Shippo test key when one exists (`shippo_test_…`; the shapes the client reads are listed at the
top of `shippo.ts`).

## How to verify by hand (needs a Shippo test key and a Zebra)

1. `supabase start && supabase db reset`, `node scripts/web-env.mjs`, then add
   `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (from `supabase status`) and either `SHIPPO_API_KEY=shippo_test_…`
   (local only) to `apps/web/.env.local`, or store the key in Vault:
   `select vault.create_secret('shippo_test_…', 'shippo_api_key:<org id>');`
2. `pnpm db:demo-owner`, `pnpm db:seed-demo`, `pnpm dev`, sign in. **Admin → Sites**: set the
   ship-from address. **Admin → Box sizes**: give a size L×W×H, packaging lb and dry ice lb.
3. Plan ship boxes at `/allocate` (method *ship*). At `/shipping` they appear under *Ship boxes
   without a shipment* → **Create shipment** (address + ship date today).
4. **Verify address** → **Shop rates** → choose a rate → **Buy labels**. With two boxes you get two
   tracking numbers. A ship date 3+ days out shows *Held — ships …* and offers **Buy Now
   (manager)** (manager PIN).
5. **Print labels** with a Zebra on a paired print agent (WP-08) set as the site's
   `shipping_label` printer — one 4×6 label per box; **Reprint labels** works any time after.
6. Tracking: set `SHIPPO_WEBHOOK_TOKEN` (the platform secret) on the server. **Admin → Sites**
   then shows this plant's own webhook URL,
   `https://<host>/api/shipping/webhook?org=<org id>&token=<HMAC(secret, org id)>`. Register it in
   the plant's Shippo account for `track_updated`. Shippo's test tracking numbers
   (`SHIPPO_TRANSIT`, …) move the shipment to *Shipped*.
   The token authenticates events for that org only. Matching runs inside that org: a
   transaction id must match exactly; without one, the tracking number must match exactly one
   of the org's parcels. A tenant never holds the platform secret or another org's credential.

## Stubs, assumptions and notes

- **WP-15 overlap (PR #67).** The I13 / I13b / I14 gate is WP-15's (`app.shipment_buy_gate`,
  generated from `rules/release.ts`, fed by `app.shipment_gate`); WP-19 consumes it unchanged.
  `20261027065000_shipment_buy_gate_from_wp15.sql` carried a **verbatim** copy of those two
  functions until #67 merged. It stays: it was applied on staging, and migrations are never
  deleted once there (docs/11 §3); #67's identical `create or replace` makes it a no-op. #67's
  migrations were renamed to `20261027230000/230100` so they sort after this PR's
  `20261027090000` (no `--include-all`).
- **Shippo key per org in Vault** (`shippo_api_key:<org id>`), as `.env.example` anticipated; env
  `SHIPPO_API_KEY` is honoured only outside production.
- **Ship-from per site** (`site.ship_from`): no address existed anywhere in the schema.
- **Cold chain** weights live in `box_size.dims` (`packaging_lb`, `dry_ice_lb`); the dry-ice
  declaration is sent as Shippo `extra.dry_ice` in lb — confirm the unit with the carrier on the
  first live label.
- **Freight quote stub:** Rate Check without the bypass returns the cheapest allowlisted rate as
  the freight quote. It is **not** written to `settlement.freight_total` (WP-14's placeholder stays
  0) — how freight is billed to an angler is not specified.
- **Cancelling a bought label** is manager-gated and noted "refund in Shippo"; v1 does not call
  Shippo's refund API.
- **Who creates shipments:** the shipping desk, from ship boxes in `planned` or `packed`. Labels
  can be bought before packing (the gate is date-based); marking shipped requires the boxes to be
  released at boxing (WP-15). If the carrier scans a shipment whose boxes were never released,
  the shipment still becomes shipped and the anomaly is written to its notes.
- The service-role client for these routes lives in `lib/shipping/http.ts`; WP-16 (PR #69) adds a
  shared `lib/supabase/service.ts` — fold into it once both are merged.
